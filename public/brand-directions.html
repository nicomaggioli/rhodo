Rhodo / Our story

Built with a firm that does the work.

We’re developing Rhodo alongside Alares Engineering, a veteran owned business, with a shared goal: help smaller firms compete for federal work with stronger proposals.

The process shapes the product.

From their workflow to better software.

A federal proposal draws on a firm’s projects, people, and qualifications. Bringing those records together takes time and judgment, especially when a small team is also delivering client work.

Working with Alares Engineering, we’re developing the software around that process: reviewing solicitations, selecting relevant experience, assembling the team, and preparing the SF330.

Their feedback helps us refine the details, so the software supports the people preparing the proposal at each step.

Built to compete.

Give every pursuit your strongest case.

Winning starts before the first draft. It means choosing work that fits, understanding the requirements, and showing why your team can deliver.

AI helps analyze opportunities, connect relevant projects and people, and prepare proposal sections. Your team checks the recommendations and makes the final decisions.

We use feedback from the proposal process to keep fine tuning the software. The aim is a more focused workflow and a stronger submission, every time your firm competes.
