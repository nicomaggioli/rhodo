The product / A closer look

Your next pursuit. All in one place.

Rhodo connects your firm’s experience to the work ahead. Here’s how a pursuit moves through the system.

01

Keep an eye on the right work.

Scheduled SAM.gov monitoring brings opportunities into your pipeline using the filters agreed with your firm.

  • NAICS codes, geography, and set asides
  • Notice details, amendments, and deadlines
  • A shared view from analyzed to accepted
02

Understand the opportunity.

AI reads the solicitation and prepares a fit assessment against your capabilities, with the scope, strengths, and open questions in one place.

  • Analysis grounded in the solicitation
  • Scoring rules for your firm
  • A human check on the scope before proceeding
03

Choose the proof. Then the people.

Review the most relevant completed projects, then the personnel with evidence of doing that work.

  • Section F recommendations based on your projects
  • Role coverage, qualifications, and alternates
  • Visible experience matrix and team approval
04

Bring the package together.

Prepare the SF330 around your approved choices and inspect each section before the final submission.

  • Section E resumes and Section F project sheets
  • Section G matrix, Section H narratives, and firm information
  • Compiled package with final review by your team

The feedback loop

Your knowledge gets another use.

Source material gives the AI its starting point.

Project records, personnel experience, and the solicitation provide the facts for recommendations and generated sections.

Saved feedback guides the next draft.

Guidance on resume wording and project descriptions can be saved and used in future generation. A preferred description does not have to be a correction you make from scratch every time.

Your decisions help refine the fit.

Decisions to pursue or decline help update opportunity scoring. Your team continues to evaluate the evidence and make the final call.

Judgment at every step

The system prepares. Your team approves.

Missing information is a prompt to check the source. Personnel recommendations are a starting point for a conversation. Your team owns the final staffing decisions, claims, and submission.

A little more room to grow.

Your next chapter starts with a conversation.
