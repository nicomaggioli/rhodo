# Asset notes

Rhodo is the founder-selected company name, inspired by rhodonite, Massachusetts’ state gemstone. The angular R logo was supplied by the founder and faithfully traced as SVG. The existing vector wordmark uses Geist.

The structural-detail and studio-worktable photographs are generated illustrative brand imagery. They do not depict customer projects. Earlier botanical reference media is not included in this repository.

The dashboard images and narrated demo use the supplied application frontend with synthetic sample records. They are interface examples, not customer results or a demonstration of backend generation speed. Original private application source and databases are not included. The narration uses one continuous ElevenLabs Roger take, with consistent pacing and normalized loudness. Captions, chapter navigation, red callouts, and feature zooms follow word timings transcribed locally. A capture artifact along the top edge is repaired in the source frames before encoding.

Geist and Inter are distributed with their SIL Open Font Licenses in `public/assets/fonts/`.
